About

I am a doctoral researcher at CNRS and Aix-Marseille University, working at the intersection of computational biology, network science, and machine learning. My research focuses on integrating multi-omics data to model mitochondrial tissue diversity, biological mechanisms, and disease-associated processes.

Research interests include multi-omics integration, network biology, mitochondrial systems biology, biological foundation models, mechanistic interpretability, and interpretable machine learning.
